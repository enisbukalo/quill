# Self-check

Re-read your architecture-research artifact and correct that artifact only. Re-open the actual
construction and wiring sites behind every ownership, boundary, dependency-direction, lifecycle,
and state-flow claim. Check both dependency directions and the full relevant lifecycle. A
declaration or annotation does not prove runtime ownership.

Remove false claims and preserve unresolved facts as explicit unknowns with the evidence searched.
Do not turn an unknown into a design decision. Do not propose a design or implementation stages;
record only verified constraints the planning phase must respect.
